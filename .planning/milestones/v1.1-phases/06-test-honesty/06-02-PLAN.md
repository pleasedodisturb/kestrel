<objective>
Make the test suite stop hiding broken imports (G-1382; TEST-01, TEST-02).

`tests/test_parse_scoring_response.py` wraps `from job_scorer import parse_scoring_response` in `try/except ImportError` followed by a module-level `pytest.skip(..., allow_module_level=True)`. The function was removed long ago, so all 19 tests have been silently skipped. This is the sibling of the `test_openai_provider.py` case that G-1348 fixed. This plan adds an AST-based guard that sweeps `tests/` and `tools/tests/` and fails on that construct and its spelling variants. It deletes the dead suite and re-pins, against the live parser `_try_parse_structured`, the 10 of its 19 cases that still describe real behaviour.

Purpose: a broken import must fail collection loudly, never look like a deliberate skip.
Output: one new guard test file, one deleted dead test file, 10 new test ids in `tests/test_json_parse_retry.py`, three commits.
</objective>

## Decisions (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- **DEC-07: delete the dead file (ticket option 2), do not relocate it.** RESEARCH Finding 2: `parse_scoring_response` has zero references outside the dead file. Its successor, `_try_parse_structured(content: str, feature: AIFeature)` in `src/career_os/ai/openrouter_provider.py`, returns a Pydantic model or None, not the old flat dict. So the file cannot be repointed without reviving a second, divergent parser that nothing calls.
- **DEC-08: account for all 19 deleted cases instead of dropping them silently (Task 3).** A scratch probe by the planner (2026-09-29) found that all 10 still-meaningful cases pass against `_try_parse_structured` today. Re-pinning them costs one small edit and turns "deleted 19 tests" into an auditable ledger.
- **DEC-09: sweep `tests/` and `tools/tests/` (RESEARCH Open Question 1).** Recurse into subpackages (property/, geo/, fuzz/, eval/, regression/) and cover every `*.py`, including conftest.py and helper modules, because they execute at collection too. The planner's scratch run of the detector design swept 215 files and found exactly one hit, `tests/test_parse_scoring_response.py:15`.
- **DEC-10: detection is AST-based and keyed on the enclosing handler, not on the call alone.** Keying on the call alone would flag the three legitimate file-presence guards, which use the identical `pytest.skip(..., allow_module_level=True)` call (RESEARCH Pitfall 1). Beyond the literal construct, the guard also flags these spelling variants of the same mask: ModuleNotFoundError, tuple handlers, a pytest alias, a from-imported skip, a broad except when the try body imports, and `pytest.importorskip` of a first-party module. Without them, the guard could be bypassed by a one-line rewrite. `pytest.importorskip` of third-party optional packages is pytest's sanctioned mechanism and is not flagged.
