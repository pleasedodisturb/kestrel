<objective>
Encode every target behaviour of the later v2.0 phases as an input-to-output mapping table that CI collects today as a strict xfail (MAP-03, D-08), Track A, inside Kestrel.

Design choice (Claude's discretion, reversible): tables are pure YAML data reviewed by the maintainer; one engine module parametrizes every row and applies `pytest.mark.v2_pending` plus `pytest.mark.xfail(strict=True, raises=NotBuilt, reason="v2.0 Phase N")` from the table's `pending: true` flag. D-08 says a phase "turns them green by removing the marker"; here the phase removes it by flipping its own table to `pending: false`, which drops both marks for that phase only. A strict xfail that passes early fails CI (verified on pytest 9.0.3 in RESEARCH.md), so pending behaviour cannot silently go green.

Profile scope (D-13, per-profile constraint): the tables are the generic target. Every row's inputs use synthetic profiles (fictional candidates, fictional companies, the example geo config), so the same rows hold for the maintainer and for the v2.1 friends' profiles. The maintainer-profile target is plan 11-01's Family B goldens (scrubbed, output-only samples of his real runs in the private pipeline), which no table reads or references.

Phase numbers follow ROADMAP.md's add order after Cut A (13): Find 14, Filter 15, Scan 16, Score 17, Track 18, Write 19, Connect 20, Apply 21. They are provisional until each `/gsd-phase add`; a renumber is a search-and-replace in the YAML `phase:` fields and the inventory pins. Phases 12 (Foundation) and 13 (Cut A) are not encoded (OQ-5): their requirements are settled and those phases write their own tests; MAP-09's citation check covers Cut A.

Purpose: each later phase turns red rows green instead of writing tests after the fact.
Output: the `v2_pending` marker, `tests/targets/` package, eight tables, the engine and the inventory meta-test.
</objective>
