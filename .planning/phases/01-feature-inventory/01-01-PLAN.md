    it reads job descriptions and your profile, then returns a score from 0-10 with a letter

    **Bullets to include (derive exact wording from source docs):**
    - AI-powered multi-factor scoring with profile-driven weights (skills, experience, job family, location, preferences)

    - Dual-score architecture: "fit" (how well you match) vs. "desire" (how much you want it)
