## Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, criticize ideas not people, and don't tolerate harassment or discrimination of any kind.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code, contact the maintainers at **<conduct@example.com>**.

Reports are read by the named maintainer(s) only and answered within **5 business days**. We take action — that may include warning, temporary mute, ban, or removal of contributions — proportional to the violation. Retaliation against reporters is itself a violation.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions, Discord, in-person events) and when an individual is officially representing the project.
