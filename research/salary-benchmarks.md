## Market Data Sources

### Germany (TPM/Product/AI Roles)

**Levels.fyi Germany (2025):**
- TPM (L5/Senior): 90-130k EUR base, 130-180k EUR TC
- TPM (L6/Staff): 120-160k EUR base, 180-250k EUR TC
- Product Manager (Senior): 85-120k EUR base
- AI/ML Engineer (Senior): 100-150k EUR base

**StepStone Germany (2025):**
- National median: 45,800 EUR
- Engineering: 58,500 EUR
- Frankfurt premium: +10-20% over national median
- Tech/Product roles: 70,000-110,000+ EUR depending on seniority

**Kununu DACH:**
- 15M+ employer reviews with salary data
- Gender pay gap insights available
- Use for company-specific research

### Frankfurt Context

- Frankfurt is Germany's financial hub, highest-paying city for many roles
- Strong international company presence (ECB, Deutsche Bank, Commerzbank tech divisions)
- Growing tech scene alongside finance
- Cost of living index: ~15% above German average

### EU Pay Transparency Directive

Coming mid-2026: German companies will be required to publish salary ranges in job postings. This will dramatically improve negotiation leverage. Watch for early adopters.

## Negotiation Framework

### Anchor Points
- Previous comp at Wolt: 125k + 40k RSU = 165k TC
- Short tenure doesn't diminish market value for the skills
- AI expertise premium is real in 2026

### Levers
1. **Base salary** -- primary lever, most predictable
2. **Equity/RSUs** -- high value at growth-stage companies
3. **Signing bonus** -- to offset gap in employment
4. **Remote work** -- has monetary value (no commute, lower CoL possible)
5. **Learning budget** -- certifications, conferences, courses
6. **Hardware/tools budget** -- Cursor, Claude Pro, etc.
